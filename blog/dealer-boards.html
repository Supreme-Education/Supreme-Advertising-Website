Dealer Boards: High-Impact Signage for Storefronts & Roadside

Supreme Advertising · Ragama, Sri Lanka

Dealer boards are built for businesses that need strong presence at the curb — tyre shops, distributors, showrooms, and service brands that rely on passing traffic.

If you do not have a traditional storefront, or you want to reinforce your main sign after dark, a well-lit or high-contrast dealer board puts your name and offer where drivers and pedestrians notice it first.

We produce boards in sizes and finishes suited to Sri Lankan weather and your mounting location, with graphics designed for quick reading at a distance.

What we offer

  • Roadside and forecourt dealer boards
  • Illuminated and non-lit options
  • Durable substrates and UV-resistant print
  • Custom shapes and brand colours

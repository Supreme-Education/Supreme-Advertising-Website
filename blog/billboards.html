Billboards: Large-Format Vinyl Faces Built to Last

Supreme Advertising · Ragama, Sri Lanka

Billboards still deliver mass reach along highways and urban corridors. Supreme digitally prints custom billboard faces in full four-colour process on 12–13 oz flexible white vinyl at up to 600 dpi so colours pop at scale.

Finishing options include bleed and pole pockets for wrapping around structures. We use solvent UV-resistant inks to slow sun fading; expect roughly two to three years of life before noticeable fade, depending on exposure.

Production typically runs one to two weeks after artwork approval. We print and deliver faces for installation on your structure — we do not broker billboard rental locations.

What we offer

  • 600 dpi digital print on 12–13 oz vinyl
  • UV-resistant solvent inks
  • Pole pockets and custom finishing
  • Building wraps and stadium-scale banners available

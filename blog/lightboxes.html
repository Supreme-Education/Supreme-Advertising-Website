Lightboxes: Illuminated Signs That Work Around the Clock

Supreme Advertising · Ragama, Sri Lanka

Lightboxes make your business easier to find at night and add polish to directory, monument, and dimensional signage. Even lighting across the face keeps logos and offers readable after dark.

Because the graphic is a replaceable panel, you can refresh campaigns without buying an entirely new sign — a practical choice for retail, restaurants, property managers, and public-facing offices.

Supreme supplies outdoor lightbox faces and complete solutions that mount to existing pylons, monument signs, or building facades across Sri Lanka.

What we offer

  • New lightboxes and retrofit faces
  • Even illumination for logos and offers
  • Swappable graphics for seasonal campaigns
  • Suited to retail, F&B, property, and institutional sites

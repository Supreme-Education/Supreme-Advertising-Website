3D Signage: Dimensional Letters That Define Your Building

Supreme Advertising · Ragama, Sri Lanka

Three-dimensional signage is the clearest way to mark your business on a facade or monument structure. Raised letters and logos cast shadows, catch light, and read from farther away than flat prints alone.

Supreme produces custom dimensional letters in metal, high-density foam, and durable plastics — with LED, halo, and backlit options so your name stays readable after sunset.

We help you choose size, depth, finish, and mounting for Sri Lankan conditions, whether the sign faces a main road or sits on a pylon at your entrance.

What we offer

  • Facade and monument dimensional letters
  • LED and backlit illumination options
  • Metal, foam, and outdoor-grade plastic
  • Installation planning for visibility and longevity

Display Units: Point-of-Sale Branding That Sells

Supreme Advertising · Ragama, Sri Lanka

Display units turn shelves and counters into branded sales tools. Headers, shelf strips, side panels, and base wraps keep products tidy while shouting your identity where purchases happen.

We build compact pharmacy counters, tyre and battery racks, water-jug stands, hand-washing stations, and trade-show-ready fixtures — fabricated to your dimensions with durable print and laminate.

From a single countertop unit to a full showroom layout, Supreme handles design, production, and branding so your products stay visible, accessible, and on-message.

What we offer

  • Countertop and freestanding POS units
  • Metal and composite rack branding
  • Illuminated shelf toppers and headers
  • Custom sizes for retail, warehouse, and events

Vehicle Branding: Turn Your Fleet Into Moving Billboards

Supreme Advertising · Ragama, Sri Lanka

Vehicle branding is one of the most cost-effective ways to advertise in Sri Lanka. Your vans, lorries, and cars travel where your customers live and work — so every trip becomes a chance to show your logo, offer, and contact details.

At Supreme Advertising in Ragama, we design and apply full wraps, partial panels, and fleet-consistent graphics using durable print and professional installation. Because the vehicles are always in motion, you reach audiences you cannot buy with a single static sign.

Whether you run one delivery van or a nationwide fleet, we align colours, typography, and layout with your brand guidelines so every vehicle looks like it belongs to the same company.

What we offer

  • Full and partial vehicle wraps
  • Fleet branding with matched layouts
  • High-resolution print and lamination for outdoor use
  • Design support from concept to installation
